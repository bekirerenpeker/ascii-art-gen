asciigen -- backdrop

The colour behind everything. Filled into the grid AND used as the padding
around the picture, so blanked cells and the border always match.

  --backdrop MODE                                               (none)

      none        black
      auto        taken from the picture itself
      #RRGGBB     exactly this colour

  --backdrop-darken F                                            (0.15)
      auto only. How far down from the picture's own colour.
      0.1  very dark     0.15  default     0.3  light enough to notice

  --backdrop-luma-threshold F                                      (40)
      auto only. Cells dimmer than this do not count toward the average.
      A 0-255 brightness, so 40 is "darker than about 15% grey".
      15  counts almost everything, backdrops come out near-black
      40  default
      90  only genuinely bright cells; strong, saturated backdrops


HOW auto PICKS

  It averages every cell bright enough to be SUBJECT rather than background,
  then darkens the result. On a blue car you get a deep teal; on a sunset, a
  dark amber.

  The threshold is the part that matters. Averaging every cell would give you
  near-black on any picture with a dark background, whatever the subject
  looked like -- so the dark cells are deliberately left out.

  It reads the colours AFTER --grid-gamma and --grid-vibrance have been
  applied, so the backdrop follows what is actually drawn. Change those and
  the backdrop shifts with them.


WHY YOU WANT ONE

  Cells that --grid-despeckle blanked still carry a background colour. Leave
  that black against a non-black canvas and they punch visible holes in the
  picture. Setting a backdrop fills them to match.
