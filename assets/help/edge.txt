asciigen -- edges

Finds edges and stamps directional characters -- / | \ -- over the glyphs that
were chosen. Off by default.

  --edge NAME                                                   (none)
      none | scharr

  --edge-threshold F                                             (0.3)
      How strong an edge has to be before anything is stamped. The
      "how much edging" dial.
      0.1  nearly everything     0.3  outlines     0.6  only the strongest

  --edge-coherence F                                            (0.55)
      How much the cell has to agree on a single direction. Rejects
      texture, where the samples point every which way and no line
      character would represent it.

      It is the fraction of the cell's edge strength pointing the same way,
      so it runs 0 to 1 and the numbers are readable: random texture lands
      near 0.3, a clean straight line near 0.9.

      0.4   lets textured cells through     0.55  default
      0.75  only unmistakable lines         above 0.9  almost nothing

  --edge-subsamples N                                              (4)
      Gradient samples per cell per axis, so 4 means 16 samples per cell.
      Cost grows with the square -- 8 is four times the work of 4.
      2  coarse and cheap     4  default     8  finer, rarely worth it


WHY IT IS OFF BY DEFAULT

  bitmask and structure already match glyph shape across the whole charset.
  Overwriting their choice with one of four characters throws detail away.

  Direction alone cannot tell ` from _ -- both are horizontal, they just sit
  at different heights in the cell. The base algorithms already know that;
  the edge pass does not.

  Turn it on for --algo ramp, for very small grids, or when you actually want
  the outlined look.


THE CHARSET DOES NOT NEED THESE CHARACTERS

  If - / | or \ are missing they get appended to the set. Existing characters
  keep their place, and it happens after selection, so the algorithm never had
  them available to pick for a cell that is not an edge.

  A blocks charset has none of the four and used to produce no edges at all.
  It now produces all four directions.
