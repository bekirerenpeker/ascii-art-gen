asciigen -- the character grid

Its size, and its colours. Everything here after the size runs AFTER glyphs are
chosen, so it changes how the result is drawn, never what was drawn.


SIZE

  --grid-width N        width in characters                (terminal width)
  --grid-height N       height in characters                 (from the shape)

  See --help sizing for how this interacts with picture size.


COLOUR

  --grid-brightness F                                            (1.0)
      Linear gain. Clips at the top.

  --grid-gamma F                                                 (1.0)
      Below 1 lifts without clipping. Usually what you want.

  --grid-vibrance F                                              (0.0)
      Saturation that boosts dull colours most and leaves already-vivid
      ones nearly alone. Flat saturation drives strong colours into
      clipping; it is the washed-out midtones that read as lifeless.

  --grid-palette NAME                                           (none)
      none | gruvbox | nord. Snaps colours to a fixed palette, matched by
      how different they look rather than by raw distance.

  --grid-palette-strength F                                      (1.0)
      Below 1 tints toward the palette instead of quantising to it.


CLEANUP

  --grid-despeckle F                                             (0.1)
  --no-grid-despeckle
      Blank cells that are both too faint AND too close to their own
      background to be worth drawing -- the scattered dark dots the
      selector leaves behind. A cell has to fail on both counts to go:
      heavy ink survives a dim colour, a bright colour survives a thin
      glyph.

      0.05  barely any     0.1  default     0.3  aggressive


WHY THE OUTPUT IS DIMMER THAN THE PHOTO

  About half as bright, and it is not a bug. A dark cell gets a sparse glyph
  AND a dark colour, so brightness is applied twice -- the picture lands near
  brightness squared.

  Brightening the SOURCE does not fix it. The selector just answers with
  denser glyphs and you end up in the same place. It has to be corrected
  here, after selection.

    --grid-gamma 0.7    recovers roughly a third of the gap
    below 0.5           stops brightening, starts washing out
