asciigen -- presets

  --preset NAME

A ready-made combination of flags. Anything you pass explicitly still wins,
whether you write it before or after the --preset. Repeatable; later presets
override earlier ones.


  photo         the tuned look. What most photos want.
                --algo structure --charset ascii
                --source-auto-levels --source-sharpen 0.6
                --grid-gamma 0.7 --grid-vibrance 0.4 --grid-despeckle 0.1
                --backdrop auto

  wallpaper-center   photo, floated in the middle of a 16:9 canvas.
                photo + --font-render-size 64 --grid-height 35
                        --image-aspect 16:9 --image-scale 0.6
                        --backdrop-darken 0.115

                A small grid so every glyph reads clearly, at 60% inside a
                canvas GROWN to 16:9 around it -- nothing is resampled, the
                shape is changed instead. The darker backdrop stops the empty
                area competing with the art.

  wallpaper-cover    photo, edge to edge.
                photo + --font-render-size 64 --grid-height 80

                Same glyph size, a much taller grid, so the picture comes out
                enormous and fills the screen. No aspect and no scale: the
                source's own shape is the canvas.

  terminal      photo, aimed at the screen.
                photo + --color truecolor --stdout

  lineart       outlines, no colour.
                --algo ramp --charset ramp
                --edge scharr --edge-threshold 0.2 --color none

  blocks        highest fidelity, least like text.
                --algo bitmask --algo-allow-background
                --charset blocks --backdrop auto
                (wants a font with Block Elements -- see --help charset)

  braille       maximum sub-cell detail.
                photo + --algo bitmask --charset braille --backdrop auto
                (needs a font with U+2800-U+28FF -- see --help charset)

  plain         characters only, nothing clever.
                --algo ramp --charset ramp --color none --no-grid-despeckle

  gruvbox       photo, snapped to the gruvbox palette.
                photo + --grid-palette gruvbox --backdrop #282828

  nord          photo, snapped to the nord palette.
                photo + --grid-palette nord --backdrop #2e3440


EXAMPLES

  asciigen photo.jpg --preset photo
  asciigen photo.jpg --preset wallpaper-center --out ./output
  asciigen photo.jpg --preset photo --grid-vibrance 0      turn one part off
